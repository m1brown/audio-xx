## Reservation

This region (and adjacent editorial surround) is reserved for possible
Phase 2 modules:

- underwriting;
- sponsorship acknowledgements;
- contextual partner messages;
- affiliate commerce modules;
- carefully governed advertising.

None of these exist yet, and none may be built inside a remediation or
operational envelope.

## Governing doctrine (binding on any future occupant)

1. **Sponsorship or payment must never alter Audio XX's assessment,
   ranking, recommendation, or editorial judgment.** This extends the
   existing F4/affiliate rule (commercial data is never evidence, never
   a scoring input) to any paid or partner content.
2. No placeholder "ad" labels or empty ad frames are ever exposed to
   users. Until a module ships, the region simply remains quiet space.
3. Any commercial module must be visually subordinate to the editorial
   voice (Editorial Doctrine v1) and clearly labelled as commercial.
4. Disclosure must derive from configuration the same way the affiliate
   footer does — copy and reality cannot diverge.
