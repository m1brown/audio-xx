# Audio XX Beta — a note before you start

Thanks for trying Audio XX. It's an audio advisor that reasons about
your *system* — how your components work together — and shows you the
evidence behind what it says. You're one of its first real users, and
the point of this beta is to find out where it helps and where it
falls short. Please don't be polite to it.

## Getting started

1. Sign in at **audio-xx.com** with the email we enrolled — pick any
   password on first sign-in; that creates your account.
2. Type your real system into the conversation, e.g.
   *"Assess my system: [your components]"* — and ask for an assessment.
3. Read what comes back — the written assessment, the system graphic,
   and the component cards with their sources.

## Then just use it naturally

Some things worth trying while you do (no need to do all of them):

- Tell it whether you actually **like** your system.
- **Disagree** with something it said.
- Describe a real **listening problem** — brightness, thin bass,
  fatigue — and see where it takes you.
- Ask what you should **change**, if anything.
- Ask about a component you're **considering buying** — "what if I
  replaced X with Y?" — then tell it you haven't bought anything.
- Ask a follow-up **without repeating** your system.
- Ask a plain factual question about one of your components.
- Save your system when it offers, and come back another day.

## What we'd love to know

Whenever something strikes you — good or bad — use the feedback box at
the bottom of the page (the comment field especially), or email
hello@audio-xx.com via "Report issue". The most valuable feedback is
specific: what you asked, what it said, and what you expected instead.

Two honest notes: conversations aren't stored, so a page reload starts
fresh (saved systems and assessment documents do persist). And if an
answer ever seems oddly generic or repetitive, tell us — that's exactly
the kind of thing we need to catch.
