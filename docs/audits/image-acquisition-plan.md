The 56 permission-ready rows are spread across **45 brands**. That is the
finding, and it argues against a broad outreach programme:

> **45 emails to gain 56 images.** Most brands yield exactly one row.

The founder's constraint — do not build an outreach programme whose
transaction cost exceeds the value of the coverage — is decided by that ratio,
not by the raw count of missing photographs.

## Recommended sequence

**Step 1 — run the two-email experiment first (Acora, Butler).**
Not for their coverage value, which is two images. For the *reply rate*, which
is the only number that decides whether any of the rest is worth doing. Both
have established identity and reserved rights, so they test permission
cleanly with nothing else confounding the result.

**Step 2 — only if Step 1 returns at least one workable yes:** Chord, then
Hegel. Seven rows for two emails is the best ratio available, and both are
high-recognition brands whose products appear in listener systems often.

**Step 3 — dCS and ARC.** Deliberately last. Each confounds the permission
question with an identity problem, so a silence from either is ambiguous —
it could mean "no" or "we do not hold that photograph". ARC may be
unobtainable: the Reference 5 is discontinued and only SE photography appears
to exist, which must not be substituted.

**Do not proceed past Step 2 on silence.** If two narrow, specific requests to
makers with established identity produce no reply, the answer to "is
manufacturer permission operationally cheap?" is no, and image coverage stays
demand-driven and rare. That is a useful result, not a failed one.

## What is NOT an acquisition target

- The 38 identity-unverified rows. Permission cannot fix these; asking a maker
  for rights to an asset we cannot identify is asking the wrong question.
- The 12 prohibited-provenance rows. Marketplace, retailer, reviewer and
  user-generated sources are excluded by doctrine, and no clearance changes
  that.
- The 9 identity-wrong rows. These stay suppressed and are kept as a
  regression class, not re-keyed from filenames.

## Standing constraints

Absence remains preferable to a wrong, substituted, unprovenanced or
unlicensed image. Never substitute a sibling product or approximate variant.
Local hosting is not permission. Identity is never inferred from a filename.
Enforcement stays staged at `identity`; moving to `full` is a founder decision
costing 144 of 156 rows.
